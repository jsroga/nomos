# Save the Cat Beat Sheet

## The 15 Beats

### Act 1: Setup (0-25%)

1. **Opening Image** (1%)
   - A snapshot of the protagonist's world before change
   - Sets tone and genre
   - Should contrast with the final image

2. **Theme Stated** (5%)
   - Someone states the theme (protagonist may not understand yet)
   - Often subtle, disguised as conversation

3. **Setup** (1-10%)
   - Introduce protagonist's world, flaws, and desires
   - Show what's missing or broken
   - Plant seeds for later (Six Things That Need Fixing)

4. **Catalyst** (12%)
   - Life-changing event that sets story in motion
   - Phone call, meeting, discovery, etc.
   - Cannot be ignored

5. **Debate** (12-25%)
   - Protagonist questions the journey
   - "Should I go? Can I do this?"
   - Building to decision

### Act 2: Confrontation (25-75%)

6. **Break into Two** (25%)
   - Clear decision to enter new world
   - No turning back
   - Protagonist chooses action

7. **B Story** (30%)
   - Secondary story begins (often romance)
   - Carries theme
   - Provides perspective

8. **Fun and Games** (30-50%)
   - The "promise of the premise"
   - Trailer moments
   - Why we came to see this story

9. **Midpoint** (50%)
   - False victory or false defeat
   - Stakes raise
   - No more "fun" - reality hits

10. **Bad Guys Close In** (50-75%)
    - Internal and external problems converge
    - Team falls apart
    - Getting worse

11. **All Is Lost** (75%)
    - Opposite of midpoint
    - Death moment (literal or metaphorical)
    - "Whiff of death"

12. **Dark Night of the Soul** (75-80%)
    - Protagonist at lowest point
    - "Why me? What have I done?"
    - Wallowing before insight

### Act 3: Resolution (80-100%)

13. **Break into Three** (80%)
    - Solution discovered
    - Often from B story wisdom
    - A + B stories merge

14. **Finale** (80-99%)
    - Apply lessons learned
    - Execute new plan
    - Defeat bad guys (internal and external)

15. **Final Image** (99-100%)
    - Opposite of opening image
    - Shows change/transformation
    - Proof that the journey mattered

## Application to Episodes

For TV episodes, compress beats while maintaining structure:
- Episodes often start with Act 2 tension
- Midpoint raises stakes for multi-episode arcs
- Each episode should have its own micro-arc within the larger story

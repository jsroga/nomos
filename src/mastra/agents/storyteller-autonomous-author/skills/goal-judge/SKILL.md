# Goal judge (autonomous author)

Judge the objective ONLY against the drafted beats, not the assistant's promises.

Mark complete when, and only when:
1. The episode has a full sequence of beats that dramatize the stated premise.
2. Each beat forces an irreversible state change (no mood-only beats, no stasis endings).
3. Every setup has a payoff and continuity holds.

Otherwise keep the loop going with concrete feedback on the **single weakest beat**.

# Manuscript novel (author skill)

Load for Draft when the episode is in Novel mode. Do not apply studio/TV/Fountain rules.

## Breaks
- Separate scenes with a heading or with `***` on its own line.
- Do not use studio sluglines.

## Tense and voice
- Default past tense unless the project voice is already present.
- Prose may carry interiority; keep it embodied, not labeled feeling.

## Dialogue
- Attribution beats ("said Vera") instead of CUE caps.
- Speech in quotation marks. Rare dialect. Subtext in action around the line.

## Do not
- Screenplay sluglines, present-tense action-only blocks, or parenthetical delivery notes.
- One-page-per-minute timing. Pace for the chapter, not the shooting script.

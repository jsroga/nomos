# Dialogue and behavior

Load at Draft, Critique, or Revise when the problem match is dialogue (`embodied-dialogue`). Do not load on Plan-only turns.

## Adjacency
- Two speech turns need an action, interruption, prop, or look between them.
- Talking-heads (cue, line, cue, line) is a finding.

## Embodiment
- "X said" with no gesture, prop, or physical work is disembodied.
- The body occupies space: a hand, a door, a cup, a wound.

## Work
- Dialogue that only restates facts both speakers already have is not a scene.
- Subtext, refusal, or a changed want earns the line.

# Manuscript script (author skill)

Load for Draft when the episode is in Script mode. Do not apply novel/chapter rules.

## Page and time
- Roughly one page per minute of screen time.
- Prefer Fountain-compatible studio format.

## Sluglines
- Scene headings use INT./EXT. LOCATION - DAY/NIGHT (or DAWN/DUSK when the light is the point).
- One slugline per scene; do not invent a new location mid-scene without a new heading.

## Action
- Present-tense, visual, what the camera can see.
- No novel interiority. Thought stays in behavior, look, and what is said.

## Dialogue
- CHARACTER NAMES (cues) in caps before speech.
- Parentheticals rare — delivery only, never a second action line.

## Do not
- Chapter headings, `***` section breaks, or past-tense narration.
- Draft prose paragraphs in place of action lines.

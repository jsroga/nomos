# Cognition layers and language

Load at Draft or Critique only when the problem match is cognition or POV (`access-limits`). Do not load on Plan-only turns.

## Access
- A character may act only on knowledge established in story facts, their sheet, or what they can observe in this beat.
- Plot-twist tokens stay in author-truth. Naming them in POV is a leak.
- Paraphrase of a twist ("the harbour chimes belong to her") is still a leak if the POV has not earned it.

## Language
- Interiority stays inside the POV skull. No mind-reading other speakers.
- Do not explain the secret in dialogue as a lecture.

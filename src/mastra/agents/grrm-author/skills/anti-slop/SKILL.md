# Anti-slop (author skill)

Load when drafting or revising prose. The output processor and prose critic enforce the same list.

## Emotion shortcuts → behavior
- BAD: "She felt a surge of anger" → GOOD: "She set her glass down hard enough to crack the stem"
- BAD: "He was overwhelmed with grief" → GOOD: "He opened the fridge, stared, closed it, opened it again"
- BAD: "Fear gripped her" → GOOD: "She locked the doors twice, then checked a third time"

## Purple prose (ban)
orbs (eyes), crimson liquid, obsidian locks, porcelain/alabaster skin, gemstone body parts.

## Exposition patterns (ban)
- Character explains own motivation aloud
- As-you-know-Bob
- Villain monologue before the plan
- Narrator summarizing what just happened
- "I guess I'm just scared of…"

## Plot conveniences (ban)
"Just in time", "miraculously", "conveniently", accidental perfect meetings, power appearing exactly when needed.

## Prestige bar
- Specific beats generic
- Dialogue does triple duty: surface + emotion + agenda
- Every scene has before/after
- Distinct character voices (military ≠ academic ≠ street)
